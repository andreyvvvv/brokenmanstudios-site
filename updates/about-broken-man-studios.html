Meet Broken Man Studios

Broken Man Studios is a one-person independent studio. QuiltMath is its first project, and this journal records how the work develops.

Why a studio page?

The name gives present and future projects one clear home without limiting the studio to a single platform, category or style of work. The public record starts with QuiltMath, but the structure is intentionally broad enough for different projects later.

How updates will work

Updates will be occasional and specific: releases, testing milestones, design decisions and useful work in progress. There is no filler schedule and no plan to turn the journal into a stream of advertising.

The first project

QuiltMath is an Android quilting calculator for binding, backing, half-square triangles and additional construction planning. It is currently moving through Google Play testing.
