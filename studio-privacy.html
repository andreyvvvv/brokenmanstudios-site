Privacy information for Broken Man Studios

Effective date: August 21, 2026

This notice covers the public Broken Man Studios website and official social media pages. QuiltMath has a separate, product-specific Privacy Policy.

Website

The website is static. It has no user accounts, contact forms, advertising trackers, analytics scripts or Meta Pixel. It does not intentionally set first-party advertising or analytics cookies.

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure may process standard technical request information such as an IP address, browser information, requested URL, date and time. This information may appear in short-lived server logs used for security, reliability and troubleshooting. Broken Man Studios does not use those logs to build advertising profiles or sell personal data.

Email

If you email Broken Man Studios, the studio receives the address and information you choose to provide. It is used to read and respond to the message, maintain necessary correspondence and protect against abuse. Do not send information you do not want included in that correspondence.

Facebook and other external platforms

The official Facebook Page is hosted by Meta. When you view, follow, react to, comment on or message the Page, Meta may process information under its own privacy terms. Broken Man Studios does not control Meta's platform-level processing.

If you voluntarily comment or send a message, the studio can see the information made available through Facebook and may use it to respond or moderate the Page. Broken Man Studios does not export that information into a separate marketing database. You can manage or delete your activity using the controls provided by Facebook.

External links

The website and social pages may link to Google Play, Google Groups, Facebook and other third-party services. Their privacy practices apply after you open those services.

Changes

This notice will be updated before the studio adds analytics, advertising pixels, mailing-list software, forms or another feature that materially changes how personal data is handled.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@brokenmanstudios.com.
